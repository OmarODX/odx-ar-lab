Gladiators in the souvenir

No placing, no resizing. The file carries a picture of the Colosseum souvenir. Open it, point the iPhone at the souvenir from above, and the gladiators appear inside it at the right size.

Open on the souvenir (iPhone)

After it opens, hold the phone about 30 cm above the souvenir, looking down, like the picture. Give it two or three seconds. If nothing appears, move slowly and keep the whole souvenir in view.
