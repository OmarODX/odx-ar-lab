Colosseum, alive

A tabletop arena with gladiators running and duelling. Tap the button, point the camera at your table, and the arena lands next to the souvenir.

iPhone or iPad: opens in AR Quick Look. No app needed. Move the phone slowly over the table until the arena appears, then pinch to resize.
Android: opens in Scene Viewer.
If the button does nothing: Open the USDZ directly

Built in Blender by ODX Studio with Claude. Round two anchors the scene to the souvenir itself.
